mitzy

Stop carrying your household in your head

Mitzy is a household task manager. It keeps track of the recurring things a home needs — HVAC filter replacements, car registration renewals, kids' health checkups, pet vaccines, tax deadlines — and tells you what's due before it slips. You don't build the list yourself; Mitzy already knows what a household like yours typically needs and surfaces each task at the right time.

Mitzy builds your list from a library of 200+ household tasks, sorts them by what matters most today, plans your week with you, guides you through big life events (moving, a new baby, a marriage, a name change), and helps you actually finish a task — local rules, deadlines, what to say on the phone, and nearby providers.

Mitzy asks for account details (email, name) and household details (home, cars, kids, pets, zip code) so it can personalize which tasks apply to you. If you sign in with Google, Mitzy uses your name and email address to create and load your account. If you connect Google Calendar, Mitzy reads your upcoming events to match them against your task list, so appointments you've already booked show as scheduled, and adds an event to your calendar when you ask it to schedule a task. Calendar access is optional.
