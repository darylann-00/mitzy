mitzy

Stop carrying your household in your head

Mitzy is a household management app. It keeps track of recurring tasks — HVAC filter replacements, car registration renewals, kids' health checkups, pet vaccines, tax deadlines, and more — and reminds you before they're due. You don't build the list yourself; Mitzy already knows what a household like yours typically needs and surfaces it at the right time.

Mitzy asks for account (email, name) and household details (home, cars, kids, pets, zip code) so it can personalize which tasks apply to you. If you connect Google Calendar, Mitzy reads upcoming events to match them against your task list — it never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.
